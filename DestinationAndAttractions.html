Destinations and Attractions
Hebacong Sea of Clouds
A breathtaking upland destination where the morning clouds float like a sea.
  • Description of the place

    • Hebacong Sea of Clouds is an eco-tourism site in Barangay Hebacong,
      Borongan City, Eastern Samar. It is known for its scenic mountain views
      and the stunning cloud formations that appear especially at dawn. The
      place also features viewing decks and visitor facilities for tourists.
  • Why it's Popular

    • It is popular because visitors can enjoy a beautiful “sea of clouds” view,
      especially in the early morning. Many people also visit because it is a
      relaxing nature spot, easy to appreciate, and a rising tourist attraction in
      Eastern Samar.
  • Description of the place

    • What makes Hebacong Sea of Clouds special is its natural cloud scenery
      that makes the mountains look like floating islands. It also stands out as
      an eco-tourism site that promotes nature appreciation and community
      development.
Calicoan Island
A tropical paradise known for its white sand beaches, clear waters, and surf.
  • Description of the place

    • Calicoan Island is a scenic island destination in Guiuan, Eastern Samar. It
      is known for its pristine beaches, lush greenery, lagoons, caves, and
      relaxing coastal views, making it a favorite spot for
      both adventure and nature lovers.
  • Why it's Popular

    • It is popular because of its beautiful beaches, surfing spots, and peaceful
      island atmosphere. Many visitors also come for swimming, island hopping,
      caving, and enjoying the island’s natural scenery.
  • Description of the place

    • What makes Calicoan Island special is its mix of natural attractions in one
      place. From white sand shores and lagoons to caves and forested areas. It is
      also known as the “Surfing Capital of the Visayas,” which adds to its charm
      and appeal.
Sulangan Church
A peaceful pilgrimage site known for faith, prayer, and devotion.
  • Description of the place

    • Sulangan Church, also known as the Shrine of St. Anthony of Padua, is a
      Roman Catholic church in Sulangan, Guiuan, Eastern Samar. It is a
      well-known religious site visited by devotees and pilgrims who come
      to pray and attend mass.
  • Why it's Popular

    • It is popular because many people believe St. Anthony of Padua grants
      prayers and intercedes for lost things, healing, and blessings. The church
      also attracts visitors because it is an important pilgrimage destination
      in Eastern Samar.
  • Description of the place

    • What makes Sulangan Church special is its strong spiritual significance
      and its reputation as a miraculous church. It is also a meaningful place
      of devotion for many Catholics, especially during feast days and special
      visits of thanksgiving.
Hilangagan Sandy Beach
A serene coastal escape with soft sands and calming ocean views.
  • Description of the place

    • Hilangagan Sandy Beach is a quiet beach destination in Eastern Samar, known
      for its long stretch of fine sand and clear coastal waters. It offers a peaceful
      environment ideal for relaxation and simple seaside activities.
  • Why it's Popular

    • It is popular among locals and visitors looking for a less crowded beach
      experience. People come to enjoy swimming, picnics, and the calm, laid-back
      atmosphere away from busy tourist spots.
  • Description of the place

    • What makes Hilangagan Sandy Beach special is its natural simplicity and
      unspoiled charm. Its tranquil setting and wide sandy shoreline make it a perfect
      place for relaxation, reflection, and enjoying nature.
Sohoton Caves and Natural Bridge Park
A natural wonder of caves, rivers, and a bridge.
  • Description of the place

    • Sohoton Caves and Natural Bridge Park is a protected natural attraction
      in Basey, Samar. It features limestone caves, underground rivers, waterfalls,
      thick forest, and a famous natural stone bridge formed by erosion over time.
      The park covers about 840 to 841 hectares and is part of the Samar
      Island Natural Park.
  • Why it's Popular

    • It is popular because of its unique scenery and adventure activities. Visitors
      often come for guided boat rides, cave exploration, and kayaking while
      enjoying the park’s dramatic limestone formations and river views.
  • Description of the place

    • What makes Sohoton special is its rare mix of natural features in one
      destination, especially its iconic natural stone bridge and cathedral-like caves.
      It is also known for its rich ecosystem and historical significance, making it
      one of Samar’s most remarkable eco-tourism sites.
Tinago Waterfalls
A hidden waterfall gem surrounded by lush forest and a turquoise pool.
  • Description of the place

    • Tinago Falls is a famous waterfall in Iligan City, Lanao del Norte. Its name
      means “hidden,” and it sits deep inside a ravine, where visitors must
      descend a long staircase before reaching the falls. The waterfall drops
      about 240 feet into a clear blue-green basin
  • Why it's Popular

    • It is popular because of its dramatic scenery and adventure-like access. Many
      visitors come to swim, take bamboo raft rides, and enjoy the cool, refreshing
      water and natural setting.
  • Description of the place

    • What makes Tinago Falls special is its secluded location and striking beauty. The
      combination of its towering cascade, forested ravine, and vibrant pool gives it a
      peaceful yet majestic feel that stands out among Philippine waterfalls.
